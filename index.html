Experience Mumbai's lively streets, iconic Gateway of India, and beautiful coastal views.

Bangalore

Discover Bangalore's pleasant weather, vibrant gardens, modern culture, and delicious food.

Duration: 5 days

Places to visit: Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Bangalore Palace, Cubbon Park, Vidhana Soudha, and Nandi Hills.

Jaipur

Explore Jaipur's grand forts, colourful markets, and magnificent royal architecture.

Duration: 5 days

Places to visit: Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, and Nahargarh Fort.

Gangtok

Enjoy Gangtok's peaceful monasteries, mountain views, and scenic Himalayan surroundings.

Duration: 5 days

Places to visit: Rumtek Monastery, Tsomgo Lake, Nathula Pass, Hanuman Tok, and MG Marg.

Relax among Lakshadweep's clear lagoons, coral reefs, sandy beaches, and tropical islands.

Duration: 7 days

Places to visit: Agatti Island, Bangaram Island, Kavaratti, Kalpeni, and Kadmat Beach.

New Delhi

See New Delhi's historic monuments, lively bazaars, grand avenues, and rich cultural heritage.

Duration: 7 days

Duration: 6 days

Places to visit: Tiger Hill, Batasia Loop, Darjeeling Himalayan Railway, Peace Pagoda, and Happy Valley Tea Estate.
